# Intraday Buy

## What it is
Same-day breakout signals on Nifty 500 stocks, generated by the Volume Profile engine. Positions are meant to be closed the same trading day.

## Where the data comes from
- **Scan engine:** `backend/stocks/services/intraday_service.py` → `get_live_signals()`
- **Universe:** Nifty 500 (`load_symbols_from_stock_table()`)
- **Trigger logic:** `_volume_profile_logic()` computes POC/VAH/VAL (Point of Control, Value Area High/Low) from 15-min candles, and fires on one of 3 patterns:
  1. **POC Flip** — price crosses the Point of Control with `vol_ratio > 1.2`
  2. **VA Breakout** — price breaks above VAH / below VAL with `vol_ratio > 1.5` (the main "momentum" trigger)
  3. **VA Rejection** — price bounces off VAL/VAH with `vol_ratio > 1.1`, filtered by NIFTY trend
- **Entry/Target/SL:** Entry = current price, Target = Entry ± ATR×2, SL = Entry ∓ ATR×0.8 (or the VAH/VAL level, whichever is tighter)
- **Cap:** 5 new signals per scan cycle (`MAX_SIGNALS_PER_SCAN`)

## Where it's stored
Django model `SignalHistory`, `category="intraday"`. Fields used: `symbol`, `signal_type` (BUY/SELL), `entry_price`, `target`, `stop_loss`, `status`, `generated_at`.

## Lifecycle
- Runs every 15 min during market hours (9:15 AM–3:30 PM) as part of `run_periodic_scanners()`.
- **Stale-signal guard:** any PENDING/ACTIVE row from a *previous* trading day is auto-cancelled at the start of each scan.
- **Cutoff 3:20 PM:** PENDING signals not yet triggered are `CANCELLED`; ACTIVE ones are `EXPIRED` at market close (P&L locked in at exit price).
- Outcome auditing (target/SL hits) handled by the shared `update_signal_outcomes()` in `live_signal_service.py`.

## What decides which ones show on the Dashboard card
The preview card query (`views.py`, `DashboardSummaryView`) is:
```python
SignalHistory.objects.filter(
    category='intraday',
    status__in=[ACTIVE, PENDING],       # only currently-live signals
).order_by('-generated_at')[:3]         # most recently generated, first 3
```
So: **only signals still open right now** (not yet hit target/SL, not expired/cancelled) qualify at all — a stock that hit its target this morning won't show here even though it's still in the database. Of those, the 3 *most recently generated* are shown. The `count` badge next to the title is the total number of live ones, not just the 3 visible. The full `/live-signals` page has no such status filter — it shows everything, with its own BUY/SELL/ACTIVE/PENDING filter buttons.

## API endpoints
- `GET /api/stocks/live-signals/` — full list, DB-only read (never triggers a scan from a page view)
- `GET /api/stocks/dashboard-summary/` → `intraday` key — top-3 preview for the Dashboard card

## Frontend
- **Preview card** (Dashboard): `SignalPreviewCard` in `Dashboard.jsx`, showing symbol, BUY/SELL badge, entry/target/SL
- **Full view:** `/live-signals` → `LiveSignalsFull.jsx` → wraps the existing `LiveSignalsTable.jsx` (filters, live 1-sec price ticker for ACTIVE/PENDING rows, 5-min auto-refresh)
